EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES
1. Apply current evidence-based strategies for the diagnosis 
and management of gastrointestinal malignancies, including 
hepatobiliary, gastroesophageal, colorectal, pancreatic, and 
neuroendocrine cancers. 
2. Evaluate the role and optimal sequencing of systemic, surgical, 
radiation, and liver-directed therapies to individualize treatment 
plans. 
3. Incorporate emerging targeted therapies, biomarker-driven 
approaches, and novel treatment modalities into clinical practice. 
4. Integrate multidisciplinary collaboration into the care of patients 
with GI cancers to improve clinical outcomes. 
5. Analyze complex clinical cases to inform decision-making and 
optimize patient-centered care.
TARGET AUDIENCE
This activity is designed for healthcare professionals involved 
in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of patients with 
gastrointestinal malignancies, including medical oncologists, surgical 
oncologists, radiation oncologists, gastroenterologists, advanced 
practice providers, nurses, pharmacists, fellows, residents, and other 
members of the multidisciplinary cancer care team.
