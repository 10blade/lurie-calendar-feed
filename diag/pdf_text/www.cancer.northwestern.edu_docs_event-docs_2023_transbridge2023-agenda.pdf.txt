The Robert H. Lurie Comprehensive 
Cancer Center of Northwestern 
University is committed to fostering 
collaborations between physicians, 
basic scientists, and population 
scientists with the ultimate goal of 
translating research discoveries to 
the clinic and community, thereby 
improving cancer patient outcomes. 
This symposium is designed to 
stimulate these collaborations 
and inform the NU community of 
opportunities to translate their 
work.
