New Treatment Options for Myelofibrosis
Course Director: Jacob Choi, MD 
Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine
Objectives: At the conclusion of this activity, participants should be able to:
• Describe clinical manifestations of myelofibrosis and the treatment options that are 
presently available.
• Describe biological processes  that limit  the success of currently used   treatment 
options for myelofibrosis patients and outline  potential strategies to overcome these 
limitations.
