This activity has been designed to address the educational needs of CLL specialists (academic medical 
center) and community-based oncologists, including hematologists/oncologists, who diagnose, treat, 
and manage patients with CLL.
Discuss appropriate treatment options for patients with CLL based on evidence-based guidelines 
and clinical trial data
Examine the pros and cons of continuous therapy and fixed-duration therapy for CLL
Outline the value of SDM when developing a treatment plan for patients with CLL
Implement SDM when discussing appropriate evidence-based treatment options for patients with CLL

PROGRAM OVERVIEW
Stay current with the rapidly evolving treatment landscape of chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL). This live, 
in-person CME activity will review evidence-based treatment approaches informed by the latest clinical 
trial data and practice guidelines, compare advantages and limitations of continuous and fixed-duration 
therapies, and explore factors that influence individualized treatment selection. Participants will also gain 
practical strategies to enhance shared decision-making (SDM), helping clinicians effectively communicate 
treatment options and partner with patients to develop personalized care plans that align with clinical 
needs, preferences, and goals.
