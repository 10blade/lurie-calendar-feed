Lurie Cancer Center Professional Education Events

Lurie Cancer Center Prostate Cancer Symposium

On Wednesday, April 10, 2019, the Robert H. Lurie Comprehensive Cancer Center of Northwestern University will host a one-day symposium bringing together investigators and medical professionals from across the country to discuss and evaluate new initiatives in prostate cancer research.

2019 Presentation Access

Event Details

Wednesday, April 10

Registration & Breakfast: 7:45 a.m. - 8:15 a.m.
Program: 8:15 a.m. - 3:00 p.m.

Robert H. Lurie Medical Research Center

303 E. Superior St., Chicago
Baldwin Auditorium

Agenda Register

View a Google Map of the event location »

Admission is complimentary but advance registration is required. Please note that space is limited.
