"Engineering Tumor-Targeting Bacteria to Overcome Barriers in Therapy-Resistant Breast Cancer"

Binita Saha
Postdoctoral Associate (Sharma Lab)
Stanley Manne Children's Research Institute
Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children's Hospital of Chicago

Solid tumors present significant barriers to effective cancer treatment, including dense extracellular matrices that restrict drug penetration and complex interactions with the immune system. This presentation will highlight Binita’s doctoral work on engineering tumor-targeting bacteria to improve intratumoral transport and therapeutic efficacy, developing perfused tumor-on-chip platforms for drug screening, and investigating bacteria–neutrophil interactions to advance treatment strategies for therapy-resistant breast cancers.
