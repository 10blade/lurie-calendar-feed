GI Oncology Symposium

Register

Join us in person on the Chicago campus

Join us in person for the inaugural Lurie Cancer Center GI Oncology Symposium, a new multidisciplinary forum dedicated to the rapidly evolving landscape of gastrointestinal cancer care.  From targeted therapies and immunotherapy to innovative surgical approaches and complex case management, this one-day CME program delivers the latest evidence and expert perspectives across the full continuum of GI malignancies. Developed by the team behind the Lurie Cancer Center's longstanding oncology education programs and featuring distinguished faculty from leading cancer centers nationwide, this inaugural symposium offers a unique opportunity to connect with colleagues, explore emerging advances, and gain practical strategies that can be immediately applied to patient care.

Accreditation Information

Target Audience

The target audience for this activity includes medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, surgical oncologists, fellows, oncology nurses and allied health professionals in gastrointestinal cancer.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply current evidence-based strategies for the diagnosis and management of gastrointestinal
    malignancies, including hepatobiliary, gastroesophageal, colorectal, pancreatic, and
    neuroendocrine cancers.
  • Evaluate the role and optimal sequencing of systemic, surgical, radiation, and liver-directed
    therapies to individualize treatment plans.
  • Incorporate emerging targeted therapies, biomarker-driven approaches, and novel treatment
    modalities into clinical practice.
  • Integrate multidisciplinary collaboration into the care of patients with GI cancers to improve clinical
    outcomes.
  • Analyze complex clinical cases to inform decision-making and optimize patient-centered care.

Accreditation Statement

The Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Continuing Medical Education (ACCME) to provide continuing medical education for physicians.

Credit Designation Statement

The Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine designates this live activity for a maximum of 6.25 AMA PRA Category 1 Credit(s)™. Physicians should claim only the credit commensurate with the extent of their participation in the activity.

Other Credits

The American Academy of Nurse Practitioners and the American Nurses Credentialing Center accept AMA PRA Category 1 Credit(s)™ from organizations accredited by the ACCME.
